Privacy Policy — Life Sim: Ultimate Reality

Last updated: September 2026

Life Sim: Ultimate Reality ("the App") is a single-player offline life simulation game. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account or login, and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or location. Game progress is saved locally on your device only.

Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and interaction data. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely through Google Play billing. We do not receive or store payment information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.
